# Claim Boundaries

1. The same-city Sao Paulo inputs improve joint demand-weather credibility but are not an enterprise deployment log.
2. Olist purchase timestamps are demand-timing proxies, not observed last-mile order-release timestamps.
3. The 12 spatial zones are calibration-only abstract proxies. They do not identify a depot, actual platform position, or observed drone route.
4. The DJI Matrice 100 data provide an external mission-energy transfer envelope. They are neither Sao Paulo telemetry nor target-aircraft validation.
5. VIP labels, platform movement, `W_3`, and `H_s` are scenario or governance constructs, not empirically estimated organisational costs.
6. The primary comparison is ETFixed minus AlwaysFixed under 60 paired holdout replications.
7. Supplementary P80, gate-ablation, inertia, window, fleet-size, and guard experiments are mechanism or boundary evidence; they do not reselect the primary policy.
8. The order-level mixed-effects model is descriptive and cannot replace scenario-level paired inference.
9. The tested evidence does not establish a stable advantage of ETAdaptive over ETFixed.
10. The ETFixed VIP difference is not confirmed by the primary Student-t interval because that interval marginally crosses zero, although the paired bootstrap interval is positive.
11. Zero infeasible execution applies to guarded simulation runs under the implemented first-slot safety check; it is not a general real-world safety certification.
